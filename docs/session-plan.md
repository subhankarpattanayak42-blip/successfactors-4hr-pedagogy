# Session Plan — SuccessFactors in 4 Hours (minute-by-minute)

> Facilitator: Subhankar Pattanayak · Room: teams of 5–6 · Keep the timer visible.

## Block A — Foundations (0:00–0:55)

| Min | What happens | Facilitator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | **Hook story.** "GlobalTech Inc. grew from 300 to 500 people in a year. Payroll broke twice, offer letters went from 3 inboxes, nobody knows headcount. The CEO gives you 4 weeks." | Tell it like a case, not a syllabus. Cold-call: "Who has seen this movie in an internship?" |
| 0:08–0:20 | **HR lifecycle in 10 minutes.** Hire → Onboard → Pay → Perform → Grow → Exit. One line each, in plain words. | Translate everything: Payroll = salary pipeline; Onboarding = account setup + welcome checklist. |
| 0:20–0:35 | **Suite tour.** SuccessFactors modules mapped to lifecycle moments: Employee Central, Recruiting, Onboarding, Performance & Goals, Learning, People Analytics. Position vs. SAP HCM on-premise, Workday, Oracle in one table. | Rule: name the module ONLY after showing the HR moment it owns. |
| 0:35–0:50 | **Live navigation demo.** Employee Central home, employee profile, org chart, one hire action. Students follow along / shout the next click. | "Make, then name" — click first, vocabulary second. |
| 0:50–0:55 | Form teams (5–6), hand out Template 1. | Count off; late joiners attach to smallest team. |

## ☕ Break (0:55–1:05)

## Block B — Employee Central data model (1:05–2:00)

| Min | What happens | Facilitator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 1:05–1:20 | **Org structures.** Org chart = computer folder tree. Position (empty bench) vs Job (generic title). Show 3 levels of GlobalTech. | Draw one branch live; teams copy the pattern. |
| 1:20–1:35 | **Effective dating.** Version-history demo: same employee, three dated records (hire → transfer → correction). "Never overwrite, always keep the date." | Ask: "What breaks if you overwrite instead of dating?" (Payroll, audits, legal.) |
| 1:35–1:55 | **Sprint 1 (teams).** Fill Template 1: mini-org for one GlobalTech department + 3 effective-dated records. TA/facilitator circulates, unblocks. | One artifact per team. Template + analogy printed on the sheet. |
| 1:55–2:00 | **Lightning share.** 2 teams × 60 seconds show their org; facilitator names one thing done well. | Praise specifically; correct privately. |

## Block C — Hire-to-perform flow (2:00–2:55)

| Min | What happens | Facilitator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 2:00–2:15 | **Requisition → candidate → offer.** Role-play: one volunteer is hiring manager, one is candidate, rest are HR ops moving the packet. | Keep it theatrical — laughter is retention. |
| 2:15–2:30 | **Onboarding checklist + goal sheet.** Welcome tasks, buddy, 30-60-90; then one SMART-ish goal written live. | "A goal a junior could act on Monday." |
| 2:30–2:50 | **Sprint 2 (teams).** Fill Template 2: complete hire-to-goal packet for a sample role (e.g. campus hire, support engineer). | Circulate; push slow teams to decide, not perfect. |
| 2:50–2:55 | **Cross-team question.** Each team poses ONE sharp question to another team's packet. | Model the first question yourself. |

## ☕ Break (2:55–3:05)

## Block D — Analytics + AI + close (3:05–4:00)

| Min | What happens | Facilitator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 3:05–3:20 | **Reading analytics.** One headcount table, one attrition slice, one 9-box. "State one decision each number supports." | 9-box = priority matrix, not astrology. Place 3 sample employees together. |
| 3:20–3:35 | **AI demo + ethics debate.** Live: AI screens 5 resumes / drafts a JD in 30 seconds. Then: "Who owns a wrong AI rejection at 2 a.m.? Should GlobalTech let AI reject candidates?" Split room, 3-min debate. | Steelman both sides. Land on: AI proposes, humans own. |
| 3:35–3:50 | **Sprint 3 + exit ticket (individual).** Template 3: place one employee in the 9-box with a one-line justification; then the exit-ticket paragraph. | This is the grade — silence the room, protect the writing time. |
| 3:50–4:00 | **Close.** Collect tickets, one-line takeaway round ("SuccessFactors is ___"), thank the host professor, share resource links. | End on energy, not admin. Follow-up links by email. |

## If things go wrong

- **Wi-Fi dies:** printed templates + whiteboard carry Blocks B–D; demo becomes narrated screenshots.
- **Running 15 min late:** cut lightning shares, never cut Sprint writing time or the exit ticket.
- **60+ students:** sprints stay team-based; exit ticket still individual (the only individual grade).
